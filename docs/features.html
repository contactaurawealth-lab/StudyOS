11 Integrated Systems.
Zero Distraction.

Every algorithm, timer, and database query in StudyOS is built to solve one specific friction in student cognition.

1. Distraction-Free AI Study Session

A guided 25-minute cycle based on cognitive science:

  • Step 1: Learn — Deep concept immersion without notifications.
  • Step 2: Recall — Active retrieval prompt test before checking notes.
  • Step 3: Practice — Timed problem solving on scratchpad.
  • Step 4: Evaluate — Honest difficulty & confidence rating.
  • Step 5: Complete — Automatic room database persistence.
File: features/practice/ui/AiStudySessionScreen.kt

2. Sidebar Study Countdown Timer & Stopwatch

Always accessible from the sidebar. Features:

  • Circular animated SVG ring with high-contrast monospace time.
  • Presets: 25m Pomodoro, 45m Deep Work, 60m Marathon, and count-up stopwatch.
  • Direct link to active Subject & Chapter to credit focus hours.
  • Native Android AlarmManager with wake-locks for 100% reliable background alerts when the timer hits zero.
File: features/timer/ui/StudyTimerScreen.kt

3. Dedicated Mock & Tuition Tests Column

Coaching quizzes and weekly tuition tests no longer clutter your major board exam list:

  • Dedicated filter pill: All, Major Exams, Mock & Tuition.
  • Subject tagging selector: links test directly to your syllabus.
  • Exact Date & native TimePickerDialog (HH:mm) for accurate scheduling.
  • Cyan-badged cards with upcoming countdown and mock score weighting.

4. Mistake Bank & Error Taxonomy

Every wrong answer has a specific root cause. StudyOS classifies mistakes into 5 cognitive categories:

  • Concept gap: Fundamental theory misunderstood.
  • Memory gap: Formula or fact decayed from memory.
  • Calculation error: Arithmetic or algebraic slip.
  • Misread question: Target variable or condition missed.
  • Careless mistake: Fatigue or rush error.
File: features/practice/ui/MistakeBankScreen.kt

5. 7-Dimension Exam Readiness Engine

Stop guessing if you're prepared. The engine evaluates:

  • 1. Syllabus completion %
  • 2. Chapter understanding depth
  • 3. Active recall prompt accuracy
  • 4. Practice session frequency & speed
  • 5. Mistake penalty (unresolved error count)
  • 6. Forgetting curve decay score
  • 7. Exam proximity weight (urgency curve)
File: domain/engine/ExamReadinessEngine.kt

6. Smart Dynamic Revision Queue

Dynamically prioritizes what you should revise today. Computes an urgency priority index based on:

  • Days elapsed since last studied chapter.
  • Historical recall stability score.
  • Proximity of linked major or mock tests.
  • Auto-elevates topics with high unreviewed mistake count.
File: domain/engine/SmartRevisionQueueEngine.kt

7. Subject Intelligence & Chapter Badges

Instantly view chapter health across all enrolled subjects:

  • Status pills: X Strong (retention >80%), Y Weak, Z Due.
  • Color-coded progress bar reflecting verified mastery.
  • Direct launch into targeted 25m AI study session from any subject card.
File: features/subjects/ui/SubjectsScreen.kt

8. Instant Global Offline Search

Unified sub-second search across your entire academic universe:

  • Search across Subjects, Chapters, Notes, Flashcards, Mistakes, and Prompts.
  • Categorized search results with direct deep links.
  • 100% offline Room SQLite query with instant keyboard debouncing.
File: features/search/ui/SearchScreen.kt

9. Dual AI Study Assistant

Reliable offline assistance + optional cloud AI power:

  • Offline Mode: Grounded heuristic study assistant that breaks down difficult formulas and study schedules without internet.
  • Cloud Streaming (Optional): Bring your own OpenAI or Gemini API key for live step-by-step Socratic problem solving.
File: domain/usecase/AiUseCases.kt

10. Non-Punitive Progress & Momentum

Traditional streak counters create anxiety when life happens. StudyOS uses positive momentum:

  • Tracks cumulative focus minutes and problems mastered.
  • No guilt notifications or streak breakage punishment.
  • Celebrates real cognitive retention over vanity app opening.
File: features/progress/ui/ProgressScreen.kt

Zero cloud lock-in • Zero subscription paywalls • Complete student data sovereignty
