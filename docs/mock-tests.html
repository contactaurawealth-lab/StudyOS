Key Advantages

Why Separate Mock Tests Matter

When major annual board exams and weekly tuition tests are mixed together, priorities get distorted.

Exact Hour & Minute Timing

Board exams only need a date. Tuition classes and mock test series occur at specific slots (e.g. 04:30 PM). StudyOS integrates Android's native TimePickerDialog for accurate countdowns.

Subject & Syllabus Linking

Select the exact enrolled subject. The test automatically connects to that subject's chapter list and active recall progress, giving you targeted revision ahead of test day.

Direct-to-Mistake-Bank Flow

The moment your tuition test is graded, click into the test to log every lost mark into the 5 error categories (Concept, Memory, Calculation, Misread, Careless) for immediate recovery.
