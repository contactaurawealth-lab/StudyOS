Installation Guide

Installing on Android (Step-by-Step)

StudyOS is distributed directly as a clean standalone APK without Google Play bloat or account requirements.

01

Download APK File

Tap the download button above or open this page on your Android phone browser (Chrome, Brave, Firefox, etc.) to download StudyOS-v1.1.0.apk.

02

Allow Unknown Apps

When prompted by Android, tap Settings and toggle on "Allow from this source" for your browser. This standard Android prompt ensures only apps you intentionally install are run.

03

Tap Install & Launch

Tap Install. Once installed, tap Open to start your first distraction-free study session immediately. No account signup, no credit card, no email required.
