How StudyOS Works.
Engine Mechanics & Guide.

Understand the cognitive principles and deterministic algorithms powering your daily study sessions.

1. The 25-Minute AI Study Session

Passive studying (re-reading notes or watching video lectures without testing) produces an illusion of competence. StudyOS enforces active cognitive retrieval:

  • Learn (10m): Review textbook notes, theorems, or formulas without checking notifications.
  • Recall (5m): Close your notes. The app prompts you to mentally reconstruct the key derivation or definitions.
  • Practice (7m): Solve an unassisted sample problem under time pressure.
  • Evaluate (3m): Grade your accuracy and log any misconceptions into the Mistake Bank.

2. Exam Readiness Algorithm Formula

Your readiness score is computed deterministically using 7 weights:

Readiness = (Syllabus × 0.30) + (Recall × 0.25) + (PracticeVelocity × 0.20) - (Mistakes × 2.5%) + ProximityBonus

If you have 5 unresolved calculation mistakes, your readiness drops by 12.5% until you re-verify those problems in a practice session.

3. Scheduling Mock & Tuition Tests

To add a tuition test or coaching mock paper:

  • Navigate to the Exams screen from the sidebar.
  • Tap the Mock & Tuition tab filter.
  • Tap the + button. Enter test title (e.g. "Kinematics Unit Quiz").
  • Select the enrolled Subject to link the test to your syllabus.
  • Pick the Date and use the native Time Picker for exact start time.

4. Categorizing Errors in the Mistake Bank

Whenever you score less than full marks in a mock test or practice quiz:

  • Open the Mistake Bank screen.
  • Add the problem text or capture a question snapshot.
  • Select the honest error cause (Concept, Memory, Calculation, Misread, or Careless).
  • When revising, filter by Concept Gap to fix theoretical misunderstandings first before exams.
