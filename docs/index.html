Frequently Asked Questions

StudyOS is a personal study operating system for Android that unifies subjects, chapters, progress tracking, spaced repetition revision, and study schedules into one calm, minimal workspace.
It is built for high school, university, and self-directed students who want structured control over what to study, where they currently stand, and what to revise next.
You can track individual subjects, chapter completion states, daily study time, flashcards due for revision, practice quiz attempts, and upcoming exam deadlines.
StudyOS includes a dedicated markdown note editor for each chapter connected directly to your flashcards and quizzes. You can use it as your primary revision notes hub or alongside your existing tools.
Yes. You can add as many subjects and chapters as you need. Each subject tracks its own progress percentage and chapter hierarchy independently.
Yes, 100%. StudyOS uses an offline-first SQLite database (Room v7) directly on your device. Your data stays on your phone, and no internet connection is required.
StudyOS uses native Android system alarms (AlarmManager). They are 100% free, run locally on your device, require zero background cloud accounts or battery drain, and automatically restore even after your phone restarts.
