# StudyOS v1.3.0 — Minimal Exam-Focused Edition

A minimal, fast, distraction-free study system designed to help students plan, revise, and excel in exams.

---

## 🎯 10 High-Value Exam Features

1. **Exam Countdown**
   - Live days remaining (e.g., *"23 days until Mathematics"*).
   - Multi-exam tracking with priority weighting and target dates.

2. **Syllabus Completion Tracker**
   - Per-subject breakdown: Total Chapters, Completed, In Progress, and Not Started.
   - Percentage completion indicators with visual progress bars.

3. **Weak Chapter Detector**
   - Automatically identifies friction chapters based on completion, revision history, and confidence.
   - Clean, objective `"Needs Attention"` tags without manipulative claims.

4. **Exam Readiness Indicator**
   - Transparent 4-pillar calculation:
     - Syllabus completion (35%)
     - Revision completion (30%)
     - Question practice completion (25%)
     - Self-confidence ratings (10%)

5. **Past Paper & Question Practice Tracker**
   - Track questions attempted, correct, incorrect, and overall accuracy.
   - Dedicated session logger with time and accuracy analytics.

6. **Mock Test Mode**
   - Timed exam simulator with subject selection, live countdown, and score breakdown.
   - Automatically logs question attempts to your practice statistics.

7. **Mistake Book**
   - Dedicated catalog for questions missed during practice or past papers.
   - Record question/topic, what went wrong, and the correct underlying concept.
   - Filter by subject and track resolution status.

8. **Revision Heatmap**
   - Minimalist calendar grid showing study days, revision sessions, and practice drills.
   - Visual consistency tracking without intrusive streaks or guilt triggers.

9. **Exam Day Mode**
   - High-focus, distraction-free dashboard activated as your exam approaches.
   - Displays exam name, countdown, completion checklist, vital formulas, and key notes.

10. **Last-Minute Revision Mode**
    - High-yield rapid revision mode designed for the final 48–72 hours before test day.
    - Aggregates important chapters, weak topics, recorded mistakes, and pending flashcards.

---

## ✂️ Removed Bloat & Simplification

- **Removed AI Chat & Tutor**: Eliminated external AI dependencies, chatbot screens, and AI study sessions.
- **Removed AudioWalk**: Stripped audio walk features to focus on core visual academic revision.
- **Removed Library & Document Parsing**: Eliminated heavy document parsers and unused library screens.
- **Removed Notification Center**: Kept study reminders strictly local, simple, and reliable via Android AlarmManager.
- **Simplified Navigation**: Consolidated navigation to 5 core destinations: **Home**, **Subjects**, **Planner**, **Revision**, **Exams**.
